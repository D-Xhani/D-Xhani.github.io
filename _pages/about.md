Hello!

I am an Economic Advisor at the Bank of England. Before that, I was an Assistant Professor of Economics at [Tilburg University](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/) (2022–2025). I received my PhD from University College London in 2022.

I work on general equilibrium models with rich heterogeneity, how to bring them to the data, and what they imply for policy.
